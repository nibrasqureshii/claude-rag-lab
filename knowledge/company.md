# Acme Software Co. — Company Overview

> **Fictional company.** Acme Software Co. and every detail below are invented
> for this RAG teaching demo. Any resemblance to a real organization is
> coincidental.

Acme Software Co. builds custom web applications and AI-assisted automation
for small and mid-sized businesses. The company pairs product discovery with
pragmatic engineering to ship software that is easy to maintain.

Acme is headquartered in Springfield, at 1200 Example Avenue, Suite 400.

## History

Acme was founded in 2012 by a team of three engineers and has grown steadily
through referrals.

## Mission

Acme exists to help growing businesses replace spreadsheets and manual
processes with reliable, well-tested software.

## Company Values

- Understand the problem before writing code.
- Ship small, ship often.
- Tests are part of the feature, not an afterthought.
- Explain technical decisions in plain language.

## Services

Acme provides technology services across four main areas:

- Web Application Development
- AI & Automation
- Data & Reporting
- Technical Advisory

## Target Clients

Acme works mainly with small and mid-sized businesses in retail, logistics,
education and professional services.

## Office Locations
