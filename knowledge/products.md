# Acme Software Co. — Service Offerings

> **Fictional company.** All content is invented for this RAG teaching demo.

Acme is a custom software firm; it does not sell packaged products. Its
offerings are delivered as client engagements across four service areas.

## Web Application Development

Design and delivery of custom web applications, from first prototype to a
production system with automated tests and monitoring.

## AI & Automation

AI-assisted features and workflow automation, such as document
summarization, smart search and internal chat assistants.

## Data & Reporting

Dashboards and reporting tools that turn operational data into clear,
decision-ready views.

## Technical Advisory

Short discovery engagements that assess a client's needs, existing systems
and risks before any build begins.

## Client Industries

Acme serves small and mid-sized businesses, mainly in retail, logistics,
education and professional services.

## Note on Sourcing

Pricing and delivery timelines are agreed per engagement and are
intentionally omitted from this demo knowledge base.
